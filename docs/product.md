# AnalyzeG - Review Marketplace Platform

AnalyzeG is a comprehensive review marketplace that connects B2C users who submit authentic product reviews with B2B companies seeking consumer insights.

## Core Value Proposition
- **B2C Users**: Limited-time payout model: get up to ₹40 instantly after authentic review verification, then get the remaining amount from your 50% share when the review is sold
- **B2B Companies**: Purchase aggregated consumer review data for market research and competitive analysis
- **Admin Portal**: Comprehensive management system for review verification, user management, and data delivery

## Key Features
- Multi-step video review submission with questionnaires
- Dual video recording (activity demonstration + pros/cons analysis)
- Advanced demographic filtering and data aggregation
- Automated payment processing for both B2C payouts and B2B purchases
- Real-time review verification workflow
- Professional data export (Power BI JSON, CSV formats)

## Target Markets
- **Primary B2C**: Indian consumers across demographics willing to provide detailed product feedback
- **Primary B2B**: Companies seeking authentic consumer insights for product development and marketing
- **Secondary**: Market research firms and competitive intelligence teams

## Business Model
- B2C users follow a dual-stage payout: up to ₹40 after authentic verification, then the remaining amount from 50% of the sale value when a company buys the review
- B2B companies pay for access to aggregated review data
- Tiered pricing based on data depth and video access levels
