About AnalyzeG

Live platform for honest reviews, clear payouts, and trusted business insights.

Our Mission

AnalyzeG helps real people share honest video reviews. Brands use these reviews to make better decisions. We keep the process simple, secure, and fair for everyone.

Why Choose AnalyzeG?

  • Secure verification to confirm real reviewers and real reviews
  • Limited-time dual-stage payout for reviewers
  • Clear analytics and filtered insights for brands
  • Simple process with transparent rules and trust checks

How Reviewer Payout Works

Limited-time model: if your review is verified as authentic, you can get up to Rs 40 instantly. If the review is sold, you get the remaining amount from your 50% share after subtracting that instant payout.
